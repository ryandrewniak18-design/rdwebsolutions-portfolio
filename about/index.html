Rules I work by

Five rules. Non-negotiable.

01

If it isn't tracked, it didn't happen.

Attribution comes before spend. Always. No campaign launches until we can trace a lead from click to booking.

02

Execution beats strategy decks.

You don't need a 40-slide presentation. You need a website that converts, ads that book appointments, and a report that tells the truth.

03

One operator, accountable for everything.

No account managers, no handoffs, no "let me check with the team." The person who builds your system is the person who answers your email.

04

Vanity metrics are a lie of omission.

Impressions and reach don't pay your rent. The only report worth reading shows spend, leads, bookings, and revenue.

05

No 12-month lock-ins.

Retainers that trap you exist because the work can't stand on its own. If the numbers stop making sense, you should be free to leave.
